Estimated hours and priority are planning inputs; they do not change actual progress.

🔁 Plan revisions

No revision is added until you confirm this choice. Revision activities remain separate from the topic's canonical completion status. Existing completed revision history is preserved; pending revisions for this topic are superseded only when you save a new plan.
Each planned revision uses this duration. The scheduler places sessions on the chosen interval or the nearest available day that fits your configured capacity and revision cutoff.
